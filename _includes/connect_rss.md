## <i class="fa-solid fa-tags"></i> Release Tracking

If you’re managing infrastructure with OpenVox, staying current with agent releases is crucial for security, performance, and access to new features.
Every Github repository has a releases RSS feed and you can use it to track our releases, such as:

* [OpenVox Agent](https://github.com/OpenVoxProject/openvox/releases.atom)
* [OpenVox Server](https://github.com/OpenVoxProject/openvox-server/releases.atom)
* [OpenVoxDB](https://github.com/OpenVoxProject/openvoxdb/releases.atom)

If RSS isn't your thing, there are other ways to subscribe to updates.
[Read about them here](/docs/tracking_releases).
